Common questions

The Street View scene won't load. Scenes stream live from Google, so the game needs an internet connection. Check your connection and tap Retry.

How do I move around? Drag the panorama to look around. Where arrows appear, tap them to walk down the street. Then tap one of the two answers before the timer runs out.

Who am I racing? An AI rival. In the Play tab you choose how strong it is.

Do I need an account? No. You get a guest name automatically.

Can I delete my data? Yes. Email us with your guest name from the Profile tab. See the Privacy Policy.
