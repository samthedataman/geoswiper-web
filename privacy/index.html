GeoSwiper

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

GeoSwiper is a geography guessing game published by DATAMAN ANALYTICS LLC ("we", "us"). You can play without creating an account. This policy explains what the game handles and why.

What the iOS app sends to us

  • Anonymous player ID. On first launch our server creates a random player ID and a guest name such as "Guest 4501". They are not tied to your name, email or Apple ID, and they let your rank carry between sessions.
  • Gameplay data. The deck you played, the rounds and answers, scores, streaks, ratings and timestamps, stored with that ID. Your guest name and rating can appear on the shared leaderboard. US States runs are scored on your device and are not sent.

What stays on your device

  • The display name you type in Profile, your recent-run history and your settings. Deleting the app removes them.
  • Notifications are local reminders scheduled on your device, only if you allow them.

What we do not collect in the iOS app

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. We do not show ads, track you across other companies' apps or websites, or sell your data.

Service providers

  • Google Maps Platform (Street View). Street View imagery comes directly from Google. Google receives your IP address and device and usage information needed to serve it, under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Render. Our servers and database run on Render, which processes requests, including your IP address, to operate the service.

The web and Android versions

The web version offers optional Google sign-in. If you use it, we store your Google name, email and profile picture with your player, and your name can appear on the leaderboard. Display names chosen in the web or Android versions can also appear on the leaderboard.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep player and gameplay data while the game operates. To have yours deleted, email us with your guest name, shown in the Profile tab, and we will remove it.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We will post any changes here and update the date above.
