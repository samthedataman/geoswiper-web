Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

GeoSwiper is a geography guessing game published by DATAMAN ANALYTICS LLC ("we", "us"). You can play without creating an account. This policy explains what the game handles and why.

What the iOS app sends to us

  • Anonymous player ID. On first launch our server creates a random player ID and a guest name such as "Guest 4501". They are not tied to your name, email or Apple ID, and they let your rank carry between sessions.
  • Gameplay data. The deck you played, the rounds and answers, scores, streaks, ratings and timestamps, stored with that ID. Your guest name and rating can appear on the shared leaderboard. US States runs are scored on your device and are not sent.

What stays on your device

  • The display name you type in Profile, your recent-run history and your settings. Deleting the app removes them.
  • Notifications are local reminders scheduled on your device, only if you allow them.

What we do not collect in the iOS app

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location. We do not show ads, track you across other companies' apps or websites, or sell your data.

Service providers

  • Google Maps Platform (Street View). Street View imagery comes directly from Google. Google receives your IP address and device and usage information needed to serve it, under the Google Privacy Policy. Your use of Google Maps features and content is also subject to the Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service.
  • Render. Our servers and database run on Render, which processes requests, including your IP address, to operate the service.
  • Vercel. This website is served by Vercel, which processes requests, including your IP address, to deliver the pages.

Google Maps on this website

The demo at the top of the home page uses Google Maps features and content (a 3D map and Street View) when your browser can run them and Google's service is available. The demo starts by itself when the top of the home page is on your screen (in some browsers it waits for your first click, tap or key press). That is when they load from Google. Your use of Google Maps features and content is subject to the current Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy; see also our Terms of Use.

To show the map and the imagery, your browser connects directly to Google. Google receives your IP address, details about your browser and device, the address of this website, and the places the demo shows you, including where you look and walk. We do not send Google your name, your email address or anything else that identifies you, and this website does not ask for your location.

If your browser asks websites to save data, the page shows our own licensed 360° photographs and makes no request to Google Maps. If Google's map fails to load, the page switches to those photographs and makes no further request to Google Maps. Nothing is requested from Google Maps before the demo starts.

The home page keeps three small notes in your browser's own storage: your best score in the demo, your choice of light or dark theme, and, until you close the tab, whether the live map worked. They stay on your device and are not sent to us.

This website loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, a separate Google service: your browser requests the font files from Google.

The web and Android versions

The web version offers optional Google sign-in. If you use it, we store your Google name, email and profile picture with your player, and your name can appear on the leaderboard. Display names chosen in the web or Android versions can also appear on the leaderboard.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep player and gameplay data while the game operates. To have yours deleted, email us with your guest name, shown in the Profile tab, and we will remove it.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We will post any changes here and update the date above.
