How it works

See it. Call it. Move on.

No fiddly map pins — just two taps and a ticking clock.

  1. 01

    Get dropped

    A real Street View panorama, anywhere on the planet.

  2. 02

    Look around

    Drag to pan 360°. Tap the arrows to walk down the street.

  3. 03

    Read the clues

    Signs, plates, architecture — then tap one of two answers.

  4. 04

    Beat the clock

    30 seconds a round. The screen goes red-hot in the final 5.
